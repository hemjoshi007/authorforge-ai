▥ Your data

All projects are stored in this browser's local storage. Make regular backups.

Restoring replaces books currently saved in this browser. A confirmation is required.

✦ Free AI — no API key needed

Use the built-in prompt generator to prepare a detailed instruction for ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude in your browser. Copy the prompt, paste it into the free AI chat, and paste the result back into your chapter.

These services have their own usage limits and policies. No AI credentials are stored in this website.

◈ Free & private

No account, subscription, tracking scripts, or third-party JavaScript libraries. For public hosting, use a static host; AI requests require the optional local server.

Your books are not automatically synced between devices. Export backups before clearing browser data.
